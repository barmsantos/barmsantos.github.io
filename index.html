ABOUT ME

I'm Bárbara Santos, a recording and mixing engineer. My work covers everything from recording and mixing to sound design and music for audiovisual projects. No two projects are quite the same, and that's exactly how I like it.

I'm one of the producers at Lost in Sound Records, a lifestyle and wellness music label specialising in relaxation, meditation, sleep and background music. I'm also a teacher and training coordinator focused on audio education, helping institutions develop courses that reflect the practical realities of studio work.

As an active member of the audio community, I have a particular interest in initiatives that support the often overlooked and underutilized work of women in the industry.

A Jane of all trades, and master of them too. Because a girl can multitask.
